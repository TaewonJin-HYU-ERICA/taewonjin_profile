# Taewon Jin

Undergraduate researcher in the Department of Photonics and Nanoelectronics at Hanyang University ERICA, working on photonic and electronic devices for next-generation semiconductor systems.

My recent work centers on silicon photonics for co-packaged optics and panel-level optical interconnects, ferroelectric/oxide-semiconductor devices, micro-LED driving circuits, and emerging memory/display device concepts.

## Research Themes

- Silicon photonics on glass for optical redistribution layers and co-packaged optics
- Ferroelectric HZO-based thin-film transistors and device applications
- Oxide-semiconductor TFTs for displays, micro-LEDs, and memory devices
- Panel-level packaging and scalable photonic/electronic integration
